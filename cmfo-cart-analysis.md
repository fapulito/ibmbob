## Executive Summary

Based on research of existing CMFO cart manufacturers, there is currently **very limited availability** of DPH-approved carts that meet the high-risk requirements for cooking from raw meat, poultry, or fish. Most existing CMFO carts are approved only for prepackaged food or limited food preparation with pre-cooked ingredients.

### Key Finding
**Revolution Carts' G-Cart (Model #GRL-LA24)** is currently the only confirmed LA County DPH-approved sidewalk grill cart explicitly designed for cooking from raw on California sidewalks under SB972 regulations.

## High-Risk CMFO Requirements Analysis

According to LA County DPH guidelines and the RFP requirements, carts for cooking from raw meat, poultry, or fish must include:

### Mandatory Equipment:
1. ✅ **3-compartment sink** OR adequate spare utensils system
2. ✅ **Hot water system** (water heater)
3. ✅ **Handwashing sink** with soap and paper towels
4. ✅ **Commercial cooking equipment** (griddle/grill rated for raw meat)
5. ✅ **Refrigeration** for raw ingredient storage
6. ✅ **Fire-resistant construction**
7. ✅ **Adequate ventilation**
8. ✅ **Proper wastewater containment**
9. ✅ **Food-grade stainless steel contact surfaces**
10. ✅ **Propane or approved fuel system**

### Compliance Requirements:
- California Retail Food Code compliance
- SB972 compliance
- LA County DPH CMFO permit approval
- Unenclosed, non-motorized design
- Manually maneuverable by single operator

## Conclusion

The market for DPH-approved CMFO carts capable of cooking from raw is **extremely limited**. Revolution Carts appears to be the only manufacturer with confirmed, existing approval, making them the lowest-risk option despite higher per-unit costs.

**To maximize cart quantity within budget while managing risk, IAC should:**
1. Secure 20-25 Revolution G-Carts immediately as program baseline
2. Issue expedited sub-RFP to qualified manufacturers for cost-competitive alternatives
3. Require proof of DPH approval or **expedited plan submission** (10 business days) by September 20
4. Award remaining units only to manufacturers who can guarantee November delivery with DPH compliance

**Realistic Procurement Targets:**
- **Conservative (all Revolution):** 45-50 carts for $985,688-$1,067,625
- **Moderate (mixed):** 45-50 carts for $950,000-$1,100,000

Procuring 50 units while staying under $1.2M budget is **entirely feasible** - even with all Revolution G-Carts ($1,067,625). With mixed manufacturers and expedited DPH approvals, 50+ units can be achieved for under $1M.
