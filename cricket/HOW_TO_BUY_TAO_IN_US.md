# How to Buy TAO (Bittensor) in the United States

Since **Binance.US doesn't list TAO**, here are your alternative options for buying TAO as a US resident:

---

## ✅ Best Options for US Customers

### 1. **Kraken** (Recommended)
- ✅ **US-friendly** (except NY, ME, WA, RI, FL)
- ✅ **Direct TAO purchase** with USD
- ✅ **Multiple payment methods**: Credit card, debit card, bank transfer (ACH)
- ✅ **Low fees** and reputable exchange
- ✅ **Can start with just $10**

**How to buy:**
1. Sign up at https://www.kraken.com
2. Complete KYC verification
3. Deposit USD via bank transfer (ACH) or card
4. Navigate to TAO/USD trading pair
5. Buy TAO

**Fees:**
- ACH deposit: Free
- Credit/Debit card: ~3.75% + $0.25
- Trading fee: 0.16% - 0.26% (maker/taker)

---

### 2. **Coinbase** (Most User-Friendly)
- ✅ **Available in all 50 states** (with some restrictions)
- ✅ **Easy to use** - best for beginners
- ✅ **Direct TAO purchase**
- ✅ **Trusted and regulated** in the US

**How to buy:**
1. Download Coinbase app or visit https://www.coinbase.com
2. Create account and verify identity
3. Add payment method (bank account or card)
4. Search for "Bittensor" or "TAO"
5. Buy directly with USD

**Fees:**
- Higher than Kraken (~2-4% for card purchases)
- Bank transfer (ACH): Lower fees
- Consider using Coinbase Pro for lower fees

---

### 3. **Crypto.com**
- ✅ **Available in most US states**
- ✅ **Mobile app focused**
- ✅ **Supports 400+ cryptocurrencies including TAO**

**How to buy:**
1. Download Crypto.com app
2. Sign up and complete verification
3. Add payment method
4. Search for TAO
5. Purchase with USD

---

### 4. **KuCoin** (No KYC Required for Basic Trading)
- ⚠️ **Not officially US-licensed** but works for US users
- ✅ **No KYC required** for withdrawals under 1 BTC/day
- ✅ **Large selection** of cryptocurrencies

**How to buy:**
1. Sign up at https://www.kucoin.com
2. Buy USDT or BTC first (with P2P or card)
3. Trade USDT or BTC for TAO
4. Withdraw TAO to your Bittensor wallet

**Note:** KuCoin operates in gray area for US users - use at your own risk.

---

### 5. **Decentralized Exchanges (DEX) - Wrapped TAO**
- ✅ **No KYC**
- ⚠️ **Only wrapped TAO (wTAO)** available
- ⚠️ **More complex** - requires wallet setup

**Option: Uniswap**
1. Set up MetaMask or another Web3 wallet
2. Buy ETH on Coinbase/Kraken
3. Send ETH to your wallet
4. Visit https://app.uniswap.org
5. Swap ETH for wTAO (wrapped TAO)
6. Bridge wTAO to native TAO (if needed)

**Note:** Wrapped TAO may not work for Bittensor registration - verify first!

## 📝 Step-by-Step: Kraken (Recommended for Most US Users)

### Step 1: Sign Up
1. Go to https://www.kraken.com
2. Click "Create Account"
3. Enter email and create password
4. Verify your email

### Step 2: Verify Identity (KYC)
1. Navigate to "Get Verified"
2. Enter personal information
3. Upload government ID
4. Take a selfie
5. Wait for approval (usually minutes to hours)

### Step 3: Deposit USD
1. Click "Funding" → "Deposit"
2. Select "USD"
3. Choose "ACH" (free) or "Wire Transfer"
4. Follow instructions to link bank account
5. Initiate deposit

**Timing:**
- ACH: 1-5 business days
- Wire: Same or next business day

### Step 4: Buy TAO
1. Go to "Trade" → "Markets"
2. Search for "TAO/USD"
3. Click on TAO/USD pair
4. Enter amount to buy
5. Review order
6. Click "Buy TAO"

### Step 5: Withdraw to Bittensor Wallet
1. Set up Bittensor wallet (btcli)
2. Get your TAO receiving address
3. On Kraken: "Funding" → "Withdraw"
4. Select "TAO"
5. Enter your Bittensor wallet address
6. Enter amount
7. Confirm withdrawal

**Important:** Test with a small amount first!
