## Top-3 reason codes: examples and classification

Only one reason code fired across all 65 clips. No second or third reason
code exists in this sample.

### 1. `endon_no_depth_landmark` (47 clips, 72.3%)

**Classification: Detection failure — depth-landmark sub-system only.**

The batter's-end wicket was found in **every** invalid clip (anchor_calibrated=True,
wicket nbars 1–3 detected). The failure is exclusively that neither secondary landmark
succeeded:

- `_measure_depth_via_bowlers_end_wicket`: looks for a second wicket-shaped cluster
  **above** the batter's-end wicket (farther down the pitch), aligned within
  `_BOWLERS_WICKET_MAX_CENTRE_OFF_FRAC=0.12*w` and with height ratio in
  `[0.10, 0.75]` of the near wicket. In broadcast end-on footage the bowler's-end
  wicket is typically blocked by the standing umpire or the bowler themselves at the
  delivery moment (the exact frame shot-selection returns).

- `_measure_depth_via_popping_crease`: uses Hough lines to find a near-horizontal
  segment below the stump base. This is the crease chalk mark, which is occluded
  by the batter's feet and pads in virtually every delivery-moment frame.

Both limitations are **structural** to broadcast end-on delivery footage — not
threshold tuning issues. The values below confirm the wicket itself is well-measured:

## Conclusion

**Root cause of valid=False: the depth-landmark sub-system fails on 72.3% of VAL clips.**

- The batter's-end wicket is found reliably on 100% of clips (anchor_calibrated=True).
- `valid` requires one additional measurement — either the bowler's-end wicket cluster
  above the batter's wicket, or the popping crease below it. Neither is visible in
  broadcast end-on delivery footage most of the time.
- This is a structural constraint of the footage, not a tuning issue: thresholds cannot
  be relaxed without introducing false-positive depth measurements that would make the
  physics path worse, not better.
- The 27.7% valid rate (18/65 clips) corresponds to clips where the bowler's-end stumps
  were unobstructed — a minority of broadcast deliveries.
- Side-on camera code was never exercised: all 65 VAL clips on disk are end-on.

**Implication for the physics path**: `CRICKET_PREDICTOR_MODE=auto` correctly keeps
physics off (valid=False → falls back to constants) for 72.3% of clips. Enabling physics
on anchor-only clips (dropping the depth-landmark requirement from `valid`) would use a
depth scale derived from a single hand-calibrated constant
(`_ENDON_DEPTH_PER_TRANSVERSE=0.094`), which carries unknown accuracy; Session 5 already
measured the physics-fallback result (14.79% vs median 26.23%), confirming the current
policy is correct.
