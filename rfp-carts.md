### Who We Are  

Inclusive Action for the City (IAC) is a non-profit community development financial institution (CDFI) whose mission is to champion working people, invest in entrepreneurs, and change systems to build an economy that works for all of us. 

IAC is implementing the Sidewalk Vending Pilot Program on behalf of the LA County Department of Economic Opportunity (DEO). This program supports street vendors across all five Los Angeles County Supervisorial Districts in accessing formalization resources, code-compliant equipment, and commissary infrastructure. 

## Purpose of this RFP 
As part of the LA County Sidewalk Vending Pilot Program and Small Business Mobility Fund, IAC is seeking a qualified manufacturer to produce “cook from raw” California Food Code-compliant mobile food carts for distribution to eligible sidewalk vendors. These carts will be disbursed through DEO’s Sidewalk Vending Cart Program, with prioritization given to vendors committed to participating in Special Vending Districts. 

This solicitation is for production only. The selected manufacturer is responsible for producing carts, with already-approved plans by the LA County Department of Public Health (DPH), that meet all requirements for a Compact Mobile Food Operation (CMFO), including the ability to cook from raw ingredients. No specific design will be provided by IAC as the approved plans must be submitted by proposers. Proposers should also demonstrate their experience producing DPH-compliant mobile food carts and submit their proposed cart specifications as part of their proposal. 

All carts produced under this contract will be inspected and approved by DPH after manufacturing and prior to distribution. IAC will work with the selected manufacturer and DEO to finalize CMFO compliance after DPH inspection and approval.

## Cart Quantity
IAC anticipates purchasing between 15 and 50 carts under this contract (all-in, inclusive of production, delivery, and any associated costs). IAC reserves the right to adjust the final cart quantity within this range based on final sale price and program needs.   

No minimum order quantity is guaranteed beyond the base range of 15 carts. IAC will not be obligated to purchase the maximum quantity. All carts must be delivered no later than November 15, 2026 regardless of order size. 

## Cart Specifications 
The cart design must be finalized and approved by DPH. Selected vendors must be able to produce carts that meet all the following requirements: 

<ul>
Fully code-compliant with the California Retail Food Code, including SB 972 
Approved by the DPH as a CMFO 
Capable of supporting cooking from raw ingredients
Mobile and readily maneuverable by a single operator 
Constructed with durable materials suitable for daily outdoor commercial use 
</ul>

<em>Proposed cart specifications must be approved by DPH prior to submission. IAC will review and approve the proposed cart specifications prior to contract execution. Any modifications to the cart after award must be submitted to IAC in writing for approval prior to production, approved by DPH, and may be subject to retraction of contract.</em>  

## Pricing and Budget Requirements 

IAC is seeking the best value for the program within the budget of $420,000 for 15 carts minimum, up to a maximum of $1,200,000 for 50 carts. Best value means the proposal that represents the optimal combination of cost, quality, safety, and demonstrated capacity to deliver, and does not necessarily mean the lowest-price bid. All pricing must be all-in, including materials, labor, quality control, taxes, and delivery to an IAC-specified location within Los Angeles County.  

Proposals that exceed $420,000 for the 15-cart minimum or $1,200,000 for the 50 cart maximum will not be considered. Within that threshold, IAC will award based on best value across all evaluation criteria.

## PROPOSAL REVIEW & SELECTION PROCESS 
### Review Committee

IAC will convene an independent Review Committee to evaluate bids.

### Evaluation Criteria

The Review Committee will evaluate all responsive proposals based on the criteria below. Cost is the primary factor. IAC intends to award a contract to a single vendor. Interviews may be conducted with top-scoring proposers. 

### Rubric | Evaluation Criteria | Weight 

1 | Cost Proposal — all-in unit price per cart evaluated in the context of overall value. Proposals above $420,000 for the 15 cart minimum or above $1,200,000 for the 50 cart maximum will not be considered. 30% 

2 | Production Capacity & Timeline — demonstrated ability to produce and deliver 15 to 50 carts to a Los Angeles County location by November 15, 2026. | 20% 

3 | Relevant Experience — demonstrated experience producing DPH compliant food carts or similar equipment; quality of proposed cart specifications. | 20% 

4 | Safety & Code Compliance— documented processes for ensuring each cart meets code compliance and is DPH inspection-ready upon delivery | 10% 

5 | References — three relevant professional references | 10% 

6 | Insurance, and Legal Compliance — understanding of and commitment to ARP funding requirements (Appendix A), insurance requirements, and other legal requirements | 5% 

7 | Financial Capability — evaluated as Acceptable or Unacceptable; IAC reserves the right to request additional information or deem a proposer nonresponsive | 5% 

Total | 100%
